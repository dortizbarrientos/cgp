# Research status and next steps

## Completed within the current model

- Functional sperm loss, successful fertilisation and offspring value are
  represented separately, with explicit finite eggs and sperm.
- An analytical limit gives an interval where accepting heterospecific sperm
  and slowing their use both improve expected reproductive output.
- Finite-pool examples retain this separation for the stated conditions.
- Retention and removal can have different later outcomes despite equal
  initial heterospecific fertilisation activity.
- Egg-number, initial-load and interaction-volume scaling are separated.
- A fixed-covariance quantitative-genetic approximation follows the joint
  response of acceptance and postmating restraint.
- Numerical checks and seven manuscript figures have been generated.

These are theoretical results and numerical checks, not empirical validation.
Novelty relative to the full equations of earlier theory still needs review.

## Proposed secondary-contact work

1. Compare the current mechanism with earlier sperm-selection and CGP theory,
   and reproduce the relevant Kustra, Servedio and Alonzo (2025) benchmark.
2. Compare relative paternity choice with absolute restriction of fertilisation.
   Only the latter directly delays total fertility at a fixed gamete state.
3. Retain explicit inheritance, migration, recombination and ancestry while
   replacing fixed maternal brood production with realised fertilisation.
4. Compare receipt schedules with the same mating number and gamete loads.
5. Separate selection against costly hybridisation from fertility and sexual
   selection; measure trait evolution and consequences for gene flow separately.
6. Test the mechanism experimentally by varying eggs, loads, receipt timing
   and future compatible supply independently.

Pollen applications require deposition, germination, tube growth and ovule
access stages. Selfing, donor production and longevity evolution are later
extensions. They have not been implemented in the current manuscript model.
